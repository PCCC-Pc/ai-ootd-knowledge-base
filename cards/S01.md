## Research question / problem
Fashion recommender systems commonly recommend items or compose outfits, but the compatibility of a recommended item or outfit can depend on the scene context. This paper proposes a scene-aware recommender system.

## Method described in the abstract
The proposed SAFRS encodes scene information and outfit information using separate attention encoders, then fuses the embeddings with a scene-aware compatibility score function.

## Findings reported in the abstract
The authors report qualitative and quantitative experiments and state that SAFRS outperformed all baselines on the evaluated metrics. Exact metrics and experiment setup are not included in the abstract record reviewed for this card.

## Wiki claims this card can support
1. Outfit recommendation may consider scene context in addition to the compatibility of clothing items.
2. Scene/context information is relevant to designing an occasion-aware recommendation workflow.
