## APA 7th
Perrett, D. I., & Sprengelmeyer, R. (2021). Clothing aesthetics: Consistent colour choices to match fair and tanned skin tones. *i-Perception, 12*(6), 1–22. https://doi.org/10.1177/20416695211053361

## Study notes
The authors examined whether observers prefer clothing colours associated with different skin tones. Two experiments presented 12 women's faces (six fair and six tanned) and asked observers to choose clothing colours. The reported observer samples were N = 96 and N = 75. Participants showed preferences for red and blue hues; cool blue was favoured for fair skin and warm orange/red for tanned skin in the tested stimuli.

## Evidence boundary and limitations
The study examined a limited set of women's faces and simulated clothing-colour choices. The abstract does not establish the validity of commercial seasonal personal-colour systems (such as four-season analysis), nor does it justify universal colour rules across all ethnicities, genders or cultures. Apply the findings narrowly to the tested skin-tone and colour-preference context.
