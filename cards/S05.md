## Study notes
The abstract describes a clothing-size recommendation approach that subdivides female body types. It is compatible with four major body types specified in a Chinese standard, then subdivides each major type using characteristic indices and attributes. The paper reports a virtual-fitting simulation and pressure-distribution analysis as part of refining recommendation criteria.

## Evidence boundary and limitations
This is a clothing-size recommendation and fit-related study, not a general guide to aesthetic body-shape styling. Its classification framework is tied to the referenced Chinese standard and should not be treated as a universal taxonomy for all populations or as a basis for judging bodies. Detailed data, performance and limitations require full-text review.
