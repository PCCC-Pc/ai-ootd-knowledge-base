# Evidence Card S08

## APA 7th
Le, T.-N., Nguyen Huu, V. L., & Tran, A. C. (2026). A personalized wardrobe management application with attention-based outfit recommendation. *Procedia Computer Science, 289*, 1104–1113. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.procs.2026.08.330

## Source quality
- Research article in *Procedia Computer Science*.
- Publisher record confirms metadata and abstract.
- Full text was not retrieved during this review pass.

## Research purpose and system
The paper presents a personal wardrobe management application integrating item management, outfit management, suggestion handling and recommendation. The abstract describes recommendations generated from a user's existing wardrobe under a specified purpose.

## Method described in the abstract
The recommendation component combines item embeddings, attention-based aggregation, multilayer perceptron prediction and ranking-based retrieval.

## Results reported in the abstract
The abstract reports a selected configuration with mean squared error 0.000062 and mean cosine similarity 0.984107 between predicted and target outfit embeddings.

## Wiki claims this card can support
1. Personal wardrobe data can serve as the candidate pool for outfit recommendation.
2. Wardrobe management and outfit recommendation can be integrated in a mobile client–server application.
3. A purpose or occasion can be specified as a condition for retrieving outfit candidates.

## Limitations / boundaries
- Full text, detailed evaluation design, participants and author-reported limitations remain pending.
- Embedding similarity and MSE do not by themselves establish user satisfaction, styling quality or real-world recommendation effectiveness.
- Do not infer weather forecasting, calendar integration or push notifications from the abstract.
