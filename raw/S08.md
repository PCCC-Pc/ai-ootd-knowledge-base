## Research scope
The paper presents a personal wardrobe management application that combines item and outfit management with outfit recommendations drawn from the user's existing wardrobe. The abstract describes an ML-based recommendation component using item embeddings, attention-based aggregation, multilayer perceptron prediction and ranking-based retrieval under a specified purpose.

## Results reported in the abstract
The abstract reports a selected configuration with mean squared error 0.000062 and mean cosine similarity 0.984107 between predicted and target outfit embeddings. These are the paper's reported model evaluation values; they should not be interpreted as user satisfaction or proof of real-world styling quality.
