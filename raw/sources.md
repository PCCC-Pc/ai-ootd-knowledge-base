## Scope notes
- S01 is abstract-level only; no dataset or numerical metric claims should be made.
- S02 studies clothing colour harmony, not four-season personal-colour diagnosis.
- S03 is limited to tested face images and simulated clothing-colour choices; it does not validate commercial seasonal-colour systems.
- S04 concerns body-shape-aware style recommendation; do not turn it into universal styling rules.
- S05 concerns clothing size/fit recommendation, not aesthetic body-shape advice.
- S06 is a context-aware recommendation study; details beyond the checked record remain pending.
- S07 is a survey, not a user trial of this repository's proposed product.
- S08 is currently abstract-level; reported embedding metrics are not user satisfaction measures.
