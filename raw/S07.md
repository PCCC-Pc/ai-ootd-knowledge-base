## Research scope
This survey reviews AI techniques used in fashion recommender systems, with a focus on image-based fashion recommendation. It discusses fashion-specific compatibility, visual features, user attributes, clothing attributes and contextual information.

## Useful distinctions for this Wiki
The article distinguishes item retrieval, complementary item recommendation, whole-outfit recommendation and capsule-wardrobe recommendation. It also describes how user, clothing and context information can contribute to recommendation design. It notes the subjective nature of fashion and the difficulty of representing outfit compatibility.

## Limitations and evidence boundary
This is a literature survey, not a controlled user trial of the AI OOTD system planned in this repository. Its discussion of weather, occasion and wardrobe recommendation describes research directions and prior systems; it does not prove that this repository's future calendar or daily-push features are implemented or effective.
