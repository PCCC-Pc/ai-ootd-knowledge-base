## Abstract-level research notes
The paper argues that outfit recommendations depend not only on item or user preferences but also on scene context. It proposes a scene-aware fashion recommender system (SAFRS). The abstract describes separate attention encoders for scene and outfit information, followed by feature fusion through a scene-aware compatibility score function. The authors report that the proposed model outperformed all baselines on the evaluated metrics in their qualitative and quantitative experiments.

## Safe use in this Wiki
This source can support the limited statement that scene context is a relevant signal for fashion outfit recommendation, in addition to item/outfit compatibility. It is relevant to occasion-based Outfit planning and the future AI OOTD recommendation logic.
